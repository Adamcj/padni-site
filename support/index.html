Support

Help with your account, connected services, privacy choices, and the pre-release Padni app.

Ask in Padni

For product help, open the app and tell Padni what is not working. Include what you expected and what happened, but never send passwords or API keys.

Open the web app

Calendar connection

If a calendar connection expires or is revoked, open Settings, find Calendar integrations, and select Connect. Padni will keep local account data separate while you reconnect.

Delete your account

Open Settings and choose Delete account. Confirming deletion removes your user-scoped data from the active service and signs out your devices.

Privacy request

Use Padni to request access, correction, export, or deletion. A dedicated public support email will be added before the public store release.
